Project Overview

Built like a real warehouse operations system, not a CRUD demo

This project demonstrates a full web application architecture for a warehouse and inventory workflow: role-based access control, catalog and supplier data, per-warehouse stock balances, a stock movement ledger, purchase order state transitions, exportable reports, and a sanitized audit trail — wired together with thin controllers and a dedicated service layer.

It is designed to read the way a production internal tool would: permission-gated screens, validated forms, soft deletes on master data, and feature tests covering both happy paths and business-rule failures.

RBAC & Permissions

Custom roles, permissions, and Gate-backed middleware protect every route and Blade view.

Service-Oriented

Thin controllers delegate validation to Form Requests and business rules to a service layer.

Ledger-Based Stock

Per-warehouse balances stay in sync with an append-only stock movement ledger.

Audit Trail

Sensitive-value-sanitized logging for authentication, catalog, stock, and purchasing actions.

Architecture Highlights

Structured the way a maintained internal tool should be

Thin controllers that delegate validation to Form Requests and business behavior to services.

A service layer for stock mutations, purchase order workflows, report queries, dashboard data, and audit logging.

Eloquent models with explicit relationships for users, roles, permissions, catalog data, warehouses, stock documents, purchase orders, and audit logs.

Permission-based route protection using custom RBAC middleware and Laravel Gates.

Feature tests for authentication, authorization, CRUD workflows, stock rules, purchase orders, reports, exports, dashboard data, and audit logging.

Audit logging for important user and business actions, with sensitive-value sanitization.

Database & ERD

Ten table groups, modeled around the stock ledger

Current stock is never stored directly on a product — balances live per warehouse in warehouse_stocks, and every change is recorded in the stock_movements ledger. Master data uses soft deletes and restrictive foreign keys; purchase orders and stock documents carry their own item tables.

Testing & Quality

Feature-tested happy paths and business-rule failures

The automated suite runs against SQLite in-memory, as configured in phpunit.xml, so test runs never touch the local MySQL development database. Coverage spans:

  • Authentication and authorization
  • Catalog, warehouse, and stock CRUD workflows
  • Stock ledger rules and purchase order state transitions
  • Reports, CSV exports, dashboard data, and audit logging
